Maa's mini recipes

Three easy ways to enjoy what's inside your pouch — exactly how we do at home.

Pure Sattu · 2 min

Sattu sharbat

  1. Stir 3 tbsp sattu into a glass of cold water.
  2. Namkeen: black salt + roasted jeera + lemon. Meetha: a spoon of gur.
  3. Mix well, drink chilled. Desi protein shake, sorted.
Litti Sattu Kit · 40 min

Litti at home

  1. Knead a soft atta dough, rest 15 minutes.
  2. Mix the kit masala with 1 tsp mustard oil + lemon; stuff into dough balls.
  3. Roast till golden and cracked, dip in ghee, serve with chokha (recipe card inside!).
Pure Sattu · 25 min

Sattu paratha

  1. Mix sattu with chopped onion, green chilli, ajwain, mustard oil.
  2. Stuff into dough balls, roll out gently.
  3. Roast on the tawa with ghee till crisp. Dahi + achaar = heaven.
